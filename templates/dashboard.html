Cross-Dataset Incomparability Notice

Evaluation metrics from NSL-KDD, UNSW-NB15, and CICIDS2017 are not directly comparable on an absolute scale. Each benchmark represents a fundamentally different operational abstraction: synthetic host connection counters (NSL-KDD), packet window statistics and synthetic flows (UNSW-NB15), and bidirectional flow duration and inter-arrival timing records (CICIDS2017). Cross-dataset validation demonstrates that models cannot be interchanged without feature mapping and domain adaptation.
